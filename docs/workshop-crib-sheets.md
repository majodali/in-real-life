# Workshop Crib Sheets

Run-day cards for the three facilitation sessions.
`launch-playbook.md` §5 is the source of truth for the *why* of every
beat; these are the cards you hold while running one. **Correct them
after every session** — the diff is part of the capture.

## Before any session — mechanics

- [ ] Dedicated workshop stack up; locality bindings set for the
      community being demoed (slot A = their home).
- [ ] Personas seeded **the day before** (Seed all personas → 50/50;
      seeding is minutes, not theater).
- [ ] The script's events added; for past events, spot-check a roster
      and a debrief actually landed (open one persona's model).
- [ ] "Open as" tabs prepared for every persona the script drives;
      fixture password written on a card.
- [ ] Time jumps rehearsed — know exactly which events go "over" at
      each advance; a wrong event unlocking mid-session derails the
      debrief beat.
- [ ] Note-taker recruited (never the facilitator). Their brief:
      verbatim confusions, not summaries.
- [ ] Feedback capture ready (the R1 channel, or paper that will be
      transcribed into it the same day).

## Session A — the member's arc (~60 min)

*Audience: founding cohort, advisors, curious locals. Goal: the
member experience lands first-hand.*

1. **Frame** (5) — what IRL is; the privacy posture up front: first
   names, no messaging, just show up.
2. **Attendees do the interview** (15) — they sign up on the workshop
   stack (locality auto-verifies) and jump straight in; that IS the
   new-member experience, so model it, don't narrate it. Facilitator
   circulates and fields questions; note-taker captures every
   confusion verbatim.
3. **Their feed, read aloud** (10) — why it's ordered this way:
   prioritization, never filtering; the effort bands; nothing hidden,
   nothing scored in front of them.
4. **RSVP as themselves** (5) — interest vs. confirm, the minimum-3
   floor.
5. **Switch to a seeded persona** ("open as", 10) — advance the clock
   past an event; debrief live. The beat: this is care, not a survey —
   taps first, everything optional.
6. **"How we understand you"** (10) — open the persona's model;
   correct something live. The beat: beliefs are the member's to fix.
7. **Close + capture pass** (5) — "would you sign up — and if not,
   what's missing?" One structured answer per attendee.

*Capture: register corrections, verbatim confusions, sign-up blockers.*

## Session B — the organizer's arc (~45 min)

*Audience: organizer-shaped people, advisors. Goal: proposing feels
first-class, not form-filling.*

1. **Frame** (5) — organizers are first-class; what IRL does for you
   (rosters, thresholds, corrections) and what it never does (spam
   your attendees, score you).
2. **Propose live** (10) — watch the shape extraction get it right or
   wrong in front of everyone; correct it at edit. The beat: derived
   is a guess, the organizer's word is authoritative.
3. **Rosters + auto-plan** (10) — interest vs. confirm; the
   minimum-attendance threshold scheduling the event by itself.
4. **Suggestions + polls** (10) — the correction channel: attendees
   shape the event without messaging.
5. **Cancel-with-reason + the organizer's debrief side** (5).
6. **Capture pass** (5) — "would you propose something — what's in
   the way?"

*Capture: proposal friction, extraction misreads, threshold
comprehension.*

## Session C — the empty room (~30 min)

*Audience: founding cohort, near soft open. Goal: convert demo into
launch.*

1. **Setup**: personas seeded, **zero events added**. The calendar is
   empty because the community hasn't happened yet — nothing on it
   betrays the catalog's existence.
2. **Frame** (5) — this is not a demo; this is the room. The app is
   waiting for the community to exist.
3. **"What should the first real one be?"** (15) — the facilitator
   proposes it live from the room's answers: title, time, place,
   minimum 3. More than one if the room has more than one.
4. **Everyone RSVPs on the spot** (5).
5. **Close** (5) — when it happens for real, the debrief is how the
   app learns; do it, and say what was true.

*Capture: the proposed events themselves — they are the seed of the
real calendar at soft open.*
