# Open Risks & Critical-Review Findings

From the consolidation critique (self-review + an independent adversarial read of all notes), extended by the scenario walkthroughs (`scenario-walkthroughs.md` — entries #19+ promoted from its findings register). **Status** is one of: `fixed` (correction applied), `decide` (needs a product/values call), `design` (needs its own design task). **Severity** is as-found — how much the design's own promises depended on the defect (for #1–18, banded from the original severity ranking). This is the honest defect list — several of these were real errors in the design.

| 19 | **Tap-spam manufactures mutual-affinity edges** (walkthroughs F8). Generosity normalisation is specified only at the rating layer (welcomer signal); co-suggestion and crew seeding consume *raw* mutual edges — so tapping everyone converts every tap received into "the strongest signal the system gets" and seeds crews cheaply. Fix direction: apply tapper-generosity weighting wherever affinity edges are *consumed*, not only in rating (interpretation-layer; capture ≠ use already permits it). | `matching.md`, `debrief.md`, `hypotheses.md` | high | `fixed` — D47: edges consumed **strength-weighted** (tapper-generosity, weaker-side combiner, co-attendance-confirmable); crew seeding accumulates weighted strength; capture stays raw; tracked as H4 |
| 20 | **Graduation is read per-group, masking the left-behind member** (walkthroughs F9). "Crew went quiet = probable graduation" applied at group granularity reads *all* members as a success — including the one the crew graduated without, who is now exactly the least-served member the distributional stance exists for. | 21 | **Nudge-stack priority unspecified: guaranteed floors vs. soft de-weights** (walkthroughs F6). | 23 | **Protective-block mechanism undesigned; principles now fixed (D50).** Safety-critical and carries legal exposure: presence-shielding across *every* surface (attendee lists, people steps, counts — an attentive blocked person comparing counts to heads), what the blocker may see of the blocked (safety need vs. reverse-surveillance by a false "victim"), mutual/contested blocks, interaction with enforcement and contributor rating, and the capture-time routing that gets a person in danger to the block tier without demanding disclosure. | `matching.md`, `user-model.md`, Group 4 | high | `design` |
| 22 | **Injection floor unsatisfiable on a thin calendar; no fallback** (walkthroughs F5). "Injection into well-fitting rooms" assumes such rooms exist; in a sparse week the floor silently fails. The corrective lever is event *creation* (surfacing unmet envelope demand to organisers / propose-your-own), which is outside the ranker and currently unlinked to it. | `matching.md`, `organizer-engagement.md` | medium | `design` |

**Coherent on one point worth stating:** the debrief's frozen-deltas-in-the-event design (LLM extracts at command time, projector re-applies stored deltas) *is* replay-safe for the delta application itself. The determinism risk was the time-relative decay layered on top (#4), now fixed.
