# Open Risks & Critical-Review Findings

From the consolidation critique (self-review + an independent adversarial read of all notes). Ranked by severity. **Status** is one of: `fixed` (correction applied), `decide` (needs a product/values call), `design` (needs its own design task). This is the honest defect list — several of these were real errors in the design.

| # | Finding | Files | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Re-derivability oversold.** Replay re-applies *frozen* deltas — it reproduces the *old* extraction, it can't improve Layer 2. Evolving the schema needs a separate **batched LLM re-extraction** over Layer-1 narrative (undesigned, uncosted) — and that can't live in the "LLM-free projector." | `projection-store.md`, `user-model.md` | `fixed` (split the two claims; re-extraction named as its own job) |
| 2 | **`OnboardingCompleted` vs `UserProfileCreated` unreconciled + import gap.** The event vocab conflicts; the import chunk carries "no history," so **imported users land with zero Layer 2/3 and no rating** — yet projection-store calls replay "the user-copy path." | `event-sourcing.md`, `onboarding-interview.md`, `projection-store.md` | `decide` |
| 3 | **Crypto-shred breaks "replay reproduces state exactly."** A shredded user's PII is unreadable, so replay can't rebuild their state; projector-on-shred is unspecified; key-management ("TBD") actually blocks onboarding (first heavy-PII event). | `event-sourcing.md`, `projection-store.md` | `fixed` (guarantee restated; projector tombstones; key-mgmt flagged as onboarding blocker) |
| 4 | **Decay clock never pinned.** Time-relative decay/recency makes replay non-deterministic and conflicts with workshop time-travel. | `projection-store.md`, `workshop-mode.md` | `fixed` (anchored to event `simulatedTime`, never wall-clock) |
| 5 | **No LLM seam in workshop/test.** AI flows can't be stubbed → tests can't be fast and robots can't run. A Group-0 foundation gap. | `workshop-mode.md`, AI-flow notes | `fixed` (LLM seam added; D37) |
| 6 | **"Contributor rating informs, never dominates" is an unbacked promise.** No bounded-influence mechanism exists, yet organiser trust, facilitator gating, and enforcement already depend on it. | `contributor-rating.md`, `organizer-engagement.md`, `matching.md` | `design→matching.md` — bounded soft nudges capped below fit + exploration floor make "never dominates" structural; nudge caps need real-data tuning |
| 7 | **Mutual-affinity co-suggestion is physically observable.** If tapping "see again: Priya" makes Priya keep appearing at my events, I can infer she tapped back — the "who liked you" signal D21 forbids, and gameable. | `debrief.md`, `user-model.md`, `matching.md` | `design→matching.md` — **reframed:** remove *our* problematic influences, don't chase unobservability (real-world effects are inherently observable and that's fine); soft/noisy nudges for UX; popularity ≠ routing power |
| 8 | **Cold-start inclusion is asserted, not solved.** Matching leans on observed signal newcomers lack; crews concentrate co-suggestion among established users, crowding newcomers out. Benefit-of-the-doubt ≠ inclusion. | `user-model.md`, `contributor-rating.md`, `debrief.md`, `matching.md` | `design→matching.md` — fit-first ranking works cold; exploration + newcomer-injection floor into *well-fitting* rooms |
| 9 | **"Positive ≠ popularity" defended only by "weakly."** Affinity-received *is* popularity; any weight penalises the shy the facet exists to protect (the liking-gap). | `contributor-rating.md`, `hypotheses.md` | `fixed` — replaced raw affinity-received with the **welcomer signal** (generosity-normalized, newcomer-weighted; composition-only); raw capture retained (capture ≠ use); tracked as hypotheses H1–H3 with kill criteria (D40) |
| 10 | **Dwelling-detection vs the zero-LLM fast path.** Proactively offering reflection requires a model call the fast path forbids. | `debrief.md`, `reflection-and-coaching.md` | `decide` (explicit-pull-only v1, or a cheap always-on classifier + re-cost) |
| 11 | **Safety "quarantine" hand-waved.** What preference signal is discarded on a conduct flag, and how, was undefined. | `debrief.md` | `fixed` (command suppresses that event's preference deltas; projector treats a flagged debrief as non-model-bearing) |

**Coherent on one point worth stating:** the debrief's frozen-deltas-in-the-event design (LLM extracts at command time, projector re-applies stored deltas) *is* replay-safe for the delta application itself. The determinism risk was the time-relative decay layered on top (#4), now fixed.
