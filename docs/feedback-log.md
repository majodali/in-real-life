# Feedback Log (FB)

The intake log (`registers-and-feedback.md` §2): every substantive
piece of design/product feedback becomes a row — source, what was
said, what it touched, what happened, and whether the answer went
back. Feedback is evidence, never a vote; a row's weight is its
merit, not its count.

Disposition vocabulary: **changed** (a register/curation edit, cited)
· **stands** (recorded reasoning answered back) · **routed** (became
a backlog/radar/open-risks/hypotheses entry, cited) · **recorded**
(its first gift was a register row that didn't exist — often combined
with another disposition).

**Sources are de-identified**: this log is public, so rows name a
role and channel ("safety advisor, round 1"), never a person. The
founder is the one exception (their rows are self-evidently theirs).

The first rows are retro-recorded from the founder review rounds this
process grew out of, and say so — the log doesn't pretend to a
cleaner history than it has.
