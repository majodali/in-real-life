To our neighbors on Bainbridge

You're invited to meet your neighbors, in real life.

in·real·life is a small, not-for-profit way to find meetups on Bainbridge Island and to say you'll be there. A coffee walk, a book swap, a low-tide morning. You confirm, and then you show up. That is the whole thing.

Why we're writing

Making friends as an adult shouldn't require an algorithm. It used to take a street, a porch, and a reason to be in the same place at the same time. Bainbridge still has the street and the porch. We are trying to supply the reason.

So we built the simplest thing we could: a short list of what is happening nearby, and a way to say you'll be there. There is no more to it than that, and we intend to keep it that way.

How it goes

First, a short conversation. When you sign up we ask a few questions: what you like, when you're free, the kind of company you enjoy. It takes a few minutes, and you can change your answers whenever you like.

Then, a look at what's nearby. We show you meetups close to where you live, and the neighbors who might be at them. If nothing suits, you can propose one of your own.

Then you go. Tap “I'll be there,” and be there. Hellos happen face to face, where they always have.

The kind of thing you might find

A Saturday coffee walk from Winslow down to the ferry. A book swap on the library lawn. A low-tide morning at Fay Bainbridge. A slow loop through the Grand Forest on a Tuesday evening.

These are examples, not a calendar. The calendar is the neighborhood's to fill.

What we leave out, and what we do instead

Only first names. We share what is needed to recognize you when you arrive: a first name and a little about you. Your last name, your address and your phone number stay yours.

No messages. There is no inbox and no way to send anyone a note. The way to talk to someone is to be where they'll be. It is slower, and that is the point.

No likes, no follows, no feed. We don't rank people or score you, and we won't send you a notification you can't ignore. What you get instead is a short list, in time order, of what is coming up.

No ads, and no one to sell you to. in·real·life is not for profit. We collect the minimum a meetup needs and nothing more, and we would rather you close the app and go outside.

Adults, and neighbors. You need to be eighteen or over, and when you sign up we check that you live nearby. This is for the island you are actually on, not the internet at large.

Saying you'll be there means being there. Confirming a meetup is a small promise. Someone is making the same one to you.

Why we check where you live

Knowing that everyone here really lives nearby is most of what makes this a place you can trust. We would rather say that plainly than promise more than we can keep.

Where we are

We are starting on Bainbridge Island, Washington, and only here for now. We will add neighborhoods one at a time, so that each stays small enough to feel like one.

We have only just begun, so there is not much on the calendar yet. It fills as neighbors propose things — a walk, a swap, a Tuesday coffee — and the first few are the ones that make it a place.

Not on Bainbridge? Sign up anyway. We will write when we reach you.

We hope to see you there.

There is nothing to build up to and no one to impress. Say you'll come, and then come.
